## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added
- On-device transcription of audio and video to `.srt` with Whisper (WhisperKit) and Parakeet (FluidAudio) models.
- One-click model download in Settings → Models, batch queue, drag and drop, and a CLI mode.
- English translation (Whisper) and on-device translation to other languages via Apple's Translation framework.
